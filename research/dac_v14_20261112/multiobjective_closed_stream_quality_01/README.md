# Archive scope

This archive extends the unchanged scientific records of `multiobjective_engineering_inventory_01` with the 13 actual states of the completed BNN stream flow. Rejected legal candidates remain recorded. `verification.json` checks route/audit/vector identities, original final quality, elapsed-time attribution, and configuration accounting.

There are 671 legal observations and 34 unique quality-complete modern configurations. Of the latter, 33 retain complete original observer measurements and one has qualified original GNU-time only. Counts do not denote independent samples. `complete_configuration_inventory.json` enumerates every counted final and distinguishes the two measurement scopes.

Only the new final carries its 7247-second elapsed time; intermediate states have no complete time. The integer-second resolution, unavailable observer wall/CPU and tree PSS, and brief overlap are explicit. GNU maximum waited-process RSS is not tree PSS. The new final has no matched timing cohort and therefore cannot establish matched-time dominance. No original missing receipt is reconstructed.

`points.csv` is a compact quality/time-scope projection. Full `points.json`, endpoint vectors and raw routes remain on the shared research filesystem; publication manifests state which large files are omitted. The CSV is not a replacement for full endpoint and provenance evidence. Historical source archives and all old receipts remain intact.
