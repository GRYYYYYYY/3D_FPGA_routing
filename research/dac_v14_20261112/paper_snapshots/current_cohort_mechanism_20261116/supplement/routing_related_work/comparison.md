# Routing contributions and comparison boundaries

This is a mechanism comparison, not a reproduced cross-paper benchmark. The
primary-source access record and exact locators are in `sources.json`.

| Prior work | Established ingredient | Relationship to this study |
|---|---|---|
| [Liu et al., 2014](https://www.jcad.cn/en/article/id/55a65d33-15d9-4ecf-99e5-27e8d9edf3a9) | Timing repair after PathFinder | Post-route critical-connection repair alone is not a novelty claim. The publisher abstract is sufficient only for that limited comparison. |
| [AIR, 2020](https://csuncle.com/uploads/aspdac2020/pdf/p338_5C-2.pdf) | Incremental retained-tree routing and multiple legal convergences | Our conditional vertical domains, per-net release and owner scopes need matched evidence. AIR retains historical congestion prices; imported price-reset controls do not establish equivalent in-router continuation. |
| [CRoute, 2019](https://biblio.ugent.be/publication/8640095) | Timing-driven connection-based routing | Connection-level work is established. We test complete selected groups and acceptance; no external CRoute speed or quality comparison has been reproduced. |
| [LaZagna, ICCAD 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.05579v4) | Flexible 3D architecture, vertical connectivity and fabric infrastructure | Our fixed-input route repairs do not establish a new fabric or area reduction. The January2026 author revision is distinct from publication year2025. |
| [Poolad et al., 2026, v3](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.06421v3) | Explicit CHANZ and multi-die lookahead | Our edge-encoded two-layer outcomes do not validate this resource model or its heuristic. Cross-architecture transfer remains to be tested. |

Current evidence supports a complete BNN resource/time tradeoff, mixed transfer
outcomes, and local matched mechanism effects. It does not prove broad superiority
of the fully integrated controller. Current STA, subtree retention, rerouting and
multi-convergence by themselves are established ingredients. The proposed combined
controller still needs its registered actual execution and fixed confirmation.

No published numerical speedup or quality ratio is imported into our main table.
The two paragraphs in `moved_local_detail.tex` were preserved verbatim from the
previous main: fewer-query core timings and the budget64 divergence history retain
their original local scope and evidence, while the main keeps their implications.
